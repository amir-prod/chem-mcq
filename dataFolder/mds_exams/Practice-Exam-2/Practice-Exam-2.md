## **Equations and Constants**

$$q = C\Delta T = mc\Delta T$$

$$\Delta E_{universe} = 0$$
  $\Delta E_{system} + \Delta E_{surroundings} = 0$ 

$$\Delta E_{system} = -\Delta E_{surroundings}$$
  $q_{system} = -q_{surroundings}$ 

$$E_{_{\!R}} = \frac{1}{2} \ mv^2 \qquad E_{_{\!P}} = mgh \qquad E_{_{\!P}} = V \ (voltage) = \kappa \ \frac{Q_1 \ Q_2}{d} \ (\kappa = 8.99 x 10^9 \ J \ m \ C^2 \ )$$

$$w = F d = F \Delta x$$
  $w = -P\Delta V$ 

$$\Delta H = \Delta E + P \Delta V \ = \ q_P \qquad \qquad q_V = \Delta E$$

$$\Delta H_{rxn} = \sum \! n \; \Delta H_{f}(products) \; - \; \sum \! m \; \Delta H_{f}(reactants) \label{eq:deltaH}$$

$$N_{A} = 6.022 x 10^{23} \ mol^{\text{--}1} \qquad \qquad h = 6.626 x 10^{\text{--}34} \ J \ s \qquad \qquad c = 2.998 x 10^{8} \ m \ s^{\text{--}1}$$

$$e = 1.602 \times 10^{-19} \text{ C}$$
  $m_e = 9.109 \times 10^{-31} \text{ kg}$   $1 \text{ Å} = 1 \times 10^{-10} \text{ m}$ 

$$R_H = 1.0968 \times 10^7 \text{ m}^{-1}$$

$$E_{\kappa} = \frac{1}{2} \text{ m } v^2$$
  $\Delta E = hv$   $v\lambda = c$ 

$$E = hv = \frac{1}{2} \text{ m } v^2 + \Phi \qquad \qquad \widetilde{v} = \frac{1}{\lambda}$$

$$\Delta E = \frac{hc}{\lambda} = hc\widetilde{\nu} \qquad \qquad p = \frac{h}{\lambda} \qquad \qquad \lambda = \frac{h}{m\upsilon} \qquad \qquad \Delta x \; \Delta p \geq \frac{h}{4\pi}$$

$$\frac{1}{\lambda} = R_H \left( \frac{1}{n_1} - \frac{1}{n_2} \right)$$
 E = 2.178x10<sup>-18</sup> J  $\left( \frac{1}{n_1} - \frac{1}{n_2} \right)$ 

$$E = (-hcR_H)\left(\frac{Z^2}{n^2}\right) = 2.178 \times 10^{-18} \text{ J}\left(\frac{Z^2}{n^2}\right) = -1313. \text{ kJ mol}^{-1}\left(\frac{Z^2}{n^2}\right)$$

$$\Delta E = -2.178 \times 10^{-18} \text{ J}\left(\frac{1}{n_f} - \frac{1}{n_i}\right)$$
