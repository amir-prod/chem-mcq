A student draws the nitrate ion, NO3^-, with nitrogen as the central atom, one N=O double bond, and two N–O single bonds. What is the formal charge on the central nitrogen atom in this Lewis structure?

- **A.** -1
- **B.** 0
- **C.** +1 ✓
- **D.** +2

**Explanation:** Formal charge = valence electrons − [nonbonding electrons + 1/2(bonding electrons)]. For the central N in NO3^-: nitrogen has 5 valence electrons, 0 nonbonding electrons, and 8 bonding electrons around it (one double bond and two single bonds). Thus, formal charge = 5 − (0 + 4) = +1.
