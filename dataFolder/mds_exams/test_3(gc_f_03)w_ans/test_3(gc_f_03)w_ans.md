$$E_{photon} = (6.626 \text{ x } 10^{-34} \text{ J} \cdot \text{s})(2.9979 \text{ x } 10^8 \text{ m} \cdot \text{s}^{-1})/(100.0 \text{ x } 10^{-9} \text{ m})$$
